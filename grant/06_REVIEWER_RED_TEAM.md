# Reviewer Red-Team

Use this document before submitting the application. The objective is to expose weaknesses a skeptical reviewer could identify.

## Technical objections

**What exactly will be built?**

The answer must identify the bounded prototype, its inputs/outputs, and what will count as a successful result.

**What is novel?**

Describe the concrete technical combination or method. Do not substitute adjectives for novelty evidence.

**Why Unreal Engine?**

Explain the direct dependency or value created by the Unreal workflow. Avoid generic statements about real-time 3D.

**What could fail?**

List the main technical failure modes, how they will be detected, and what fallback or reduced-scope outcome remains credible.

## Evidence objections

**Can the claimed performance improvement be reproduced?**

A reviewer should be able to identify the baseline, test set, environment, metric, and result.

**Which claims are already demonstrated?**

Separate current evidence from future targets.

**Are any percentages or absolute claims unsupported?**

Remove them or mark them as targets/assumptions until measured.

## Budget objections

**Why is each requested cost necessary?**

Trace every material expense to a work package and deliverable.

**Could a smaller award still produce value?**

Maintain a defensible reduced/MVP scenario.

## Applicant credibility objections

**Does the proposal overstate project maturity?**

Do not describe planned capabilities as existing capabilities.

**Does the proposal depend on credentials, partnerships, customers, revenue, or adoption that are not evidenced?**

Those statements must be sourced or removed.

## Final reviewer test

A skeptical reviewer should be able to answer all of these questions from the submission without guessing:

1. What problem is being solved?
2. Why does the approach matter?
3. What will the grant pay for?
4. What will exist at the end?
5. How will success be measured?
6. What evidence supports current claims?
7. What are the main risks?
8. Why is the requested amount justified?
