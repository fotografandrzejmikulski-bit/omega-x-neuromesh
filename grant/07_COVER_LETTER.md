# Cover Letter — Epic MegaGrants

## Working draft — final submission gates remain open

Dear Epic MegaGrants Team,

I am applying for support to develop and validate OMEGA-X / NeuroMesh, a focused technical project built around Unreal Engine workflows. The project combines two closely related goals: improving geometry-processing automation for real-time 3D work and introducing explicit governance and safety controls around AI-assisted actions.

The purpose of the requested funding is not to present an already-complete platform. It is to finance the work required to turn the current architecture and source-implemented vertical slice into a demonstrable, measurable prototype with documented limitations and reproducible evidence.

The funded work is organized around a bounded development program: define the governance and geometry-processing specifications, implement the highest-value prototype workflow, establish a baseline, integrate and validate it in Unreal Engine, benchmark it against predefined tasks, analyze failures, and package the resulting evidence for external review.

The project is designed around Unreal Engine relevance rather than as a generic AI platform. The intended outcome is a concrete demonstrator that can show what the workflow does, how it performs, where it fails, and what remains to be developed.

I have deliberately separated verified facts, source claims, measurements, targets, assumptions, and hypotheses in the project documentation. This is important because the value of the proposal should come from the work itself and from evidence generated during development, not from unsupported market or performance claims.

The requested funding will therefore be tied directly to the work packages, deliverables, validation activities, and required development resources. The final amount will be based on the actual defensible cost basis and the applicable MegaGrants requirements at the time of submission.

Thank you for considering the project.

Sincerely,

Andrzej Mikulski
