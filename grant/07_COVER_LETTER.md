# Cover Letter — Epic MegaGrants

## Working draft

Dear Epic MegaGrants Team,

I am applying for support to develop and validate OMEGA-X / NeuroMesh, a focused technical project built around Unreal Engine workflows. The project combines two closely related goals: improving geometry-processing automation for real-time 3D work and introducing explicit governance and safety controls around AI-assisted actions.

The purpose of the requested funding is not to present an already-complete platform. It is to finance the work required to turn the current concept and architecture into a demonstrable, measurable prototype with documented limitations and reproducible evidence.

The funded work is organized around a bounded development program: define the governance and geometry-processing specifications, implement the highest-value prototype workflow, establish a baseline, test the resulting system, benchmark it against predefined tasks, analyze failures, and package the evidence for external review.

The project is designed around Unreal Engine relevance rather than as a generic AI platform. The intended outcome is a concrete demonstrator that can show what the workflow does, how it performs, where it fails, and what remains to be developed.

I have deliberately separated verified facts, source claims, measurements, targets, assumptions, and hypotheses in the project documentation. This is important because the value of the proposal should come from the work itself and from evidence generated during development, not from unsupported market or performance claims.

The requested funding will therefore be tied directly to the work packages, deliverables, validation activities, and required development resources. The final amount will be submitted only after the applicable MegaGrants rules and current funding limits have been checked immediately before submission.

Thank you for considering the project.

Sincerely,

Andrzej Mikulski
