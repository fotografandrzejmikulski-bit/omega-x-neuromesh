## Verified program facts

Epic's official MegaGrants page states that Cycle 2 of 2026 runs from June 29 through December 11, 2026, with the submission window from June 29 through September 4, 2026, closing at 11:59 PM ET. The review period is listed as September 5 through December 4, 2026, with notices December 7–11, 2026.

Epic describes MegaGrants as investments supporting promising projects that may otherwise lack sufficient funding to fully realize them. The program explicitly includes smaller teams, solo developers, and innovators using Epic technology. Eligible categories shown by Epic include indie games, UEFN experiences, moving from another engine, and other projects that advance real-time 3D or improve life for the 3D community.

`FULL DEFENSIBLE PROJECT COST` and `CURRENT VERIFIED PROGRAM LIMIT`
